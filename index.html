1st-Remote-Session-Manager-Pro

Control and shadow RDP sessions from one PowerShell window

PowerShell 7 RDP session manager: list users and sessions, shadow view-only or with full control, disconnect, log off and message users, with auto-elevation and self-update.

What it does

  • Lists every session with user, state and client address
  • Shadow modes: view-only or full control
  • Disconnect, log off and broadcast messages to users
